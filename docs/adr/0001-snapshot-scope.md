# Snapshots cover one store and two product categories

Snapshots read only the official English web ticket store (usjticketing.com), and only its Studio Pass and Express Pass categories. The Japanese store (store.usj.co.jp) was left out because it sits behind a bot-screening queue and its terms were never reviewed. The Extras and Regional categories were left out because the project exists to study Dynamic Pricing, and in a sample taken on 2026-11-14 Regional prices were identical on every Visit Date and Extras prices were mostly so, which makes them unlikely to show any.

## Consequences

- A day that was not captured can never be recovered. If the scope is widened later, the history of the added products starts on that day.
- Prices are never cross-checked against the Japanese store, so the published page must name its source.
- The category choice rests on Date-Based Pricing seen in a single day's data. Whether any category shows Dynamic Pricing was unknown when this was decided.
